Loadout

public class Loadout

A saved Inventory Setups preset, and the bank work to put the player into it: worn gear, the inventory (slot by slot), the rune pouch and the bank slot locks. It also answers whether the player matches the preset right now, can pre-pot from the bank, and can bank whatever is not part of the preset.

Make and use it on a script thread. Every method may wait, and waits do nothing on the client thread, so none of these methods works there.

Every log line starts with [InventorySetup:<preset name>]; session logs are searched by that tag.
