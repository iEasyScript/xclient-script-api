DepositBox

public class DepositBox

The bank deposit box interface: opening and closing it, depositing inventory and worn items, and finding and walking to the nearest box the account may use.

Deposit actions report true once the menu entry or button click was sent; they do not prove the game accepted it. Waits go through Global, so on the client thread they return at once.
