Spellbooks

public enum Spellbooks

The four spellbooks, how to tell which one is active, and how to switch between them.

Ancient and Lunar are reached by praying at their altar (and praying there again returns to the standard book). Arceuus is joined and left by talking to Tyss at the Dark Altar.

This enum must not depend on Spells, CombatSpells or the skill calculator's magic actions during class initialisation: those refer back to these constants.
