Boat

public class Boat implements WorldEntity, IEntity

A sailing boat the player is standing on: the game's WorldEntity plus sailing actions.

Get one from BoatCache.getLocalBoat(), Boats.local() or BoatNavigator.getBoat(). Whether the player is "on the boat" is decided once, when the wrapper is made: isOnBoat is simply "a real entity was wrapped". Fetch a fresh boat after boarding or leaving.

Threads: every WorldEntity accessor reads the entity on the client thread and falls back to a neutral value (null, 0, false) when that read fails. The actions click and wait, so call them from a script thread; on the client thread they act once and do not wait.
