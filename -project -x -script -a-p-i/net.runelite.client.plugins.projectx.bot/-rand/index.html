Rand

public class Rand

Random numbers with human-looking shapes: skewed, truncated and log-normal draws, click points and reaction times.

The exact shape of each distribution matters more than the code: these values decide how timings and click spots look from outside, so each method documents its procedure. Every method is static and safe to call from any thread. Nothing here touches the game.
