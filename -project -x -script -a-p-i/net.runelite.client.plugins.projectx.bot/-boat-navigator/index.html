BoatNavigator

public class BoatNavigator

Sailing: steering a boat to a destination. Sails the player's boat to a place, the way Walker walks the player there.

Long trips follow the sea lanes: every stored port-to-port route (PortPaths) is a known-safe line of water, and together they make a network the boat can be routed across with a shortest-path search. The ends of the trip (from the boat to the network, and from the network to the destination), and anything closer than a scene away, are planned on the loaded scene itself: the sea is the tiles a player cannot stand on and nothing is built on, and the boat is kept a tile clear of the shore so it goes round headlands and islands instead of into them.

The boat is steered in the game's 16 headings, at a point some way ahead on the planned line, and a boat that stops making progress is turned towards the most open water and tried again.

Walking calls end up here on their own: Walker.walkTo sails when the player is on a boat and the destination is at sea, and goes ashore first when it is on land. Call step once per loop pass from a script that runs its own loop, or sailTo to block until the boat gets there.

All methods are for script threads, not the client thread.
