BreakPreparation

public final class BreakPreparation

Cooperative preparation before a break handler pauses, stops, or logs out a script.

A plugin that must leave a dangerous area before a break registers a Handle on start, checks isRequested from its normal non-blocking loop, reaches safety over ordinary loop iterations, then calls ready. It closes the handle on stop, including hot reload. Both break handlers wait until every registered owner is ready. Closing an unready owner during a request, or a 120-second timeout, cancels that break instead of claiming the player is safe. With no participants registered, break behaviour is unchanged.
