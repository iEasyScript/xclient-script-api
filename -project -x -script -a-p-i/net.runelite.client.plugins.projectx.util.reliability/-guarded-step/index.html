GuardedStep

public final class GuardedStep

One step of a script, described before it runs: what must hold first, what to do, how to tell it worked, and how hard to try. Made by step; nothing happens until run. 


ActionResult r = reliability().step("Deposit logs")
    .require(Preconditions.bankOpen())
    .action(() -> Bank.depositAll("Logs"))
    .verify(() -> !Inventory.hasItem("Logs"), 3_000)
    .run();
